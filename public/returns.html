Returns & Refunds

We want you to feel good about what you picked.

7-day shade swap

For eligible colour products, request one shade exchange within 7 days of delivery. Products must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging. Contact our care team with your order number and the shade you would like instead. Exchanges are subject to availability and hygiene requirements.

Damaged, missing or incorrect items

Let us know within 48 hours of delivery and include your order number and clear photographs of the parcel and product. We will review the issue and explain the available replacement or refund options.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it is issued to the original payment method through the payment provider. Processing and bank credit times vary. Cash-on-delivery orders will be handled by our support team to arrange an appropriate refund method.

Start a request

Email care@saanjhbeauty.example. Opened cosmetics cannot be returned for hygiene reasons except when the item is damaged or incorrect.
