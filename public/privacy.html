Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Information we collect

To fulfil an order, we collect your name, email address, Indian mobile number, shipping address, chosen products and order/payment status. We use this information for order processing, customer support, fraud prevention, legal obligations and transaction emails.

Payments

Online payment details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay. Saanjh Beauty does not receive, store or have access to full card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs or netbanking credentials. Razorpay's privacy practices apply to payment information.

Sharing and retention

Order information is shared only with service providers needed to process payments, host the store, deliver orders or send transactional email, and when required by law. Order records are retained for accounting, legal and support purposes; access to administrative records is restricted.

Your choices and contact

You may request access, correction or deletion where applicable by contacting privacy@saanjhbeauty.example. Some order records must be retained to meet legal obligations.

Security

We use access controls, HTTPS in production and security measures appropriate to a small online store. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
